Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you book.

How is the fare calculated?

Where a preset route distance is available, the website shows an estimate using ₹15/km. Toll and parking are extra. Final fare is confirmed on WhatsApp.

What is the Akkalkot Round Trip fare?

Pune ↔ Akkalkot Round Trip is shown as the special ₹9,000 package. Toll/parking, night charge and driver allowance apply separately where applicable.

Can I book a village or local destination?

Yes. You can type the village, locality, landmark or destination manually even when it is not in the suggestions.

How do I confirm my booking?

Submit the form. A Booking ID is generated and the complete booking summary is sent to WhatsApp for availability and final-fare confirmation.
