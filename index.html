FAQ

How is the fare calculated?

The site uses a configurable estimate of ₹15/km plus a base amount. Toll and parking are extra. Final fare is confirmed before the trip.

What is the Akkalkot package?

Pune ↔ Akkalkot Round Trip is configured as a ₹9,000 special package.

How does trip verification work?

After confirmation, the owner starts the trip by recording the start odometer photo, driver photo and customer OTP. At trip end, the end odometer photo is recorded and the final bill is calculated.
