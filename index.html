Frequently Asked Questions

What are the local rental rates?

4/40 ₹1,800 • 6/60 ₹2,300 • 8/80 ₹2,800 • 12/120 ₹3,700 for Maruti Ertiga CNG. Extra KM ₹17 and extra hour ₹200.

How is outstation fare calculated?

For routes with a stored estimate, the website uses the displayed road KM × ₹15/km. Round trip uses the estimated distance for both directions. Toll and parking are extra. Final fare is confirmed before booking.

How much advance is required?

15% of the displayed package/estimated fare is shown as the booking-confirmation advance.

What if my village or destination is not in the list?

Enter the exact place in the destination box. If there is no reliable stored estimate, the website does not invent a KM or price; Kautuk confirms the route and final fare on WhatsApp.

Can I book airport or tour packages?

Yes. Select Airport Transfer or Tour Package in the booking form and send the request on WhatsApp for final fare and availability.
