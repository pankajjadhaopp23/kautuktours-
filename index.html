Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the fare calculated?

Local packages use the selected package price. Outstation estimates use the configured Pune-to-destination distance at ₹15/km, with toll/parking extra. Final fare is confirmed on WhatsApp.

What happens after I submit a booking?

The booking is saved with a unique Booking ID, then WhatsApp opens with the complete booking summary so Kautuk can check availability and confirm it.

Can I book a village or landmark?

Yes. Select the district and correct taluka, then type the exact village, hotel or landmark.

Will I get a final bill?

Yes. The trip system is designed to calculate the final bill from actual start/end odometer readings and applicable extra charges.
